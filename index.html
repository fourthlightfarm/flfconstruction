Our Services

About Us

Fourth Light Farm Construction, LLC provides general contracting and custom tile solutions to clients across Southern NH and the immediate area. Our team brings 13 years of hands-on experience in the field, with services also covering rot repair and architectural drafting.

We're proud to support Fourth Light Farm, a nonprofit dedicated to sustainable agriculture and soil health — construction work that gives back to the community.

Our Services

General contracting and specialty services for Southern NH and the surrounding area.

General Contracting

Full project management and trade coordination, overseeing your build from start to finish.

Custom Tile

Custom tile work for kitchens, bathrooms, and living spaces, installed with an eye for detail.

Rot Repair

Diagnosing and repairing wood rot and structural damage before it becomes a bigger problem.

Architectural Drafting

Drafting services to help plan your project before the first wall goes up.

Building With a Purpose

10% of your project cost is donated to our affiliated nonprofit, Fourth Light Farm, to support education and scholarships for students pursuing regenerative agriculture education and careers.
